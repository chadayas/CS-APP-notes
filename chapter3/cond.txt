In the first two instructions of main we are simply placing our local variables on the stack.
the compiler movs our values to 32 bit registers, implying that we may need extra space for any
computations of our value 0x7fff (32,767), this can be seen with the movswl instructions.

At the call site we jump to the overflow() function. The registers %edi and %esi hold 32,767 and 1
respectively. in there we do some computations on these registers.
